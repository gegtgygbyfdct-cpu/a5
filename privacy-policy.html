Legal Guidelines

Privacy Policy

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

At the House of Bagvioraix, safeguarding client privacy is an absolute foundation.
This Privacy Statement details how we collect, utilize, and protect your private information.
By engaging our concierge booking services or registry, you consent to these terms.
We guarantee that we will never sell, swap, or trade client details with marketers.
Please review this legal policy carefully to understand our luxury database management procedures.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide directly when registering for our showroom catalogs.
This includes your full name, primary email address, shipping details, and billing information.
Additionally, our system records technological details such as IP addresses, cookie preferences, and sessions.
This collection is performed solely to personalize your bespoke bag order and verify transactions.
We preserve client information only for as long as is necessary to complete restoration services.

3. Utilization of Sourced Data

The data we gather is used to deliver exceptional, hand-crafted products to your doorstep.
We process payments, coordinate shipping logistics, and manage custom monogram details using your files.
We also distribute announcements regarding private showroom openings, collections, and restoration workshops.
You can easily remove your email from our VIP registry by clicking unsubscribe below.
Our administrative team evaluates web traffic to maintain fast, secure website performance globally.

4. Database Sharing and Protections

Bagvioraix does not share client files with external marketing agencies or credit companies.
Information is shared exclusively with verified agencies who assist in completing custom handcraft orders.
These include gold-plating metal specialists, secure shipping couriers, and credit card processing systems.
All third-party services are contractually bound to maintain absolute database confidentiality at all times.
We may also disclose data if legally required by state authorities during official investigations.
Your technical safety remains our highest priority during all collaborative digital commerce transactions.

5. Technical Safety and Audits

We implement industry-standard database encryption protocols to prevent unauthorized portal access or changes.
All transactions containing billing details use secure sockets layer encryption pathways during checkout.
Our workshop servers are maintained in high-security facilities with continuous electronic control systems.
We execute regular technical security audits to patch potential system security weaknesses immediately.
While we strive for absolute protection, no digital transmission method is fully error-free.
We recommend maintaining secure passwords and keeping your account access keys completely private.

6. Client Rights and Preferences

You possess the right to view, correct, or delete any personal details we store.
To exercise these rights, submit a formal request to our concierge support desk.
We will process your request within thirty business days in accordance with regulations.
You may also choose to block web cookies through your web browser preferences panel.
Doing so might limit access to custom color selectors on our collection explorer page.

7. Contact and Statement Modifications

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any given time.
All revisions will be published immediately on this page with an updated modification date.
If you have questions regarding this statement, please reach out to our team.
You can write to our atelier at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.
Alternatively, call our official concierge support helpline at +1-888-777-5845 for immediate queries.
