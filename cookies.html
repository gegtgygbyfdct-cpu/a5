Cookie Policy

1. Purpose of Cookie Deployments

Cookies are minute script files written to your hard drive by web servers.
They are widely used to make web applications run faster, smoother, and securely.
These files help us remember your monogram selections, catalogs, and currency preferences.
They contain identifier codes that link your local browser sessions with our database.
You can manage or completely disable these files through your browser options pane.

2. Sourcing and Customizations

Bagvioraix uses cookies to verify client accounts and prevent fraud during transactions.
These files remember your bag custom configurations, catalog view filters, and shopping bag lists.
We also employ analytical tracking tools to evaluate how users navigate through our website.
This collective information helps us constantly improve server speeds, layouts, and image sizes.
We never utilize cookies to track your behavior across unrelated external shopping platforms.

3. Categories of Web Cookies We Use

Required cookies are essential for the fundamental operation of our checkout and reservation portal.
Performance cookies collect anonymized statistics regarding error reports, page load speeds, and link clicks.
Functional cookies remember your visual settings, including language translations, font sizes, and sound levels.
Targeting cookies are utilized by search platforms to show relevant luxury bag ads to you.
All of these cookies combine to deliver a premium and responsive digital storefront experience.
You can inspect each category individually through your browser developer panel preference window.

4. Modification of Cookie Settings

Most web browsers automatically accept cookies by default to keep website navigation fluent.
However, you can configure your browser to decline cookies or send a notification.
Please refer to your web browser documentation for step-by-step preference modification guidelines.
Note that disabling required cookies will immediately interrupt boutique purchases and account logins.
You will need to manually key in transaction details during each atelier visit.

5. Cookie Policy Revisions

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our server infrastructure.
All modifications will be posted immediately on this page with a revised date header.
We recommend checking this page regularly to stay informed about our tracking improvements.
Your continued use of our platform constitutes acceptance of all modifications published here.
